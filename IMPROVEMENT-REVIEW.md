# Improvement pass after author feedback

18 October 2026 · Baseline: commit `59727bd`

This pass addresses the author's criticism of repetitive graphics and over-simplified replacement lessons. It is not an assertion that the book is objectively the best, or that technical checks prove editorial excellence.

## Actual changes

- Replaced the repeated three-card panels with eight topic-specific visual motifs across 24 graphics: communication exchanges, a stepped goal path, observation/question/choice, a balance, connected tools with a review loop, interweaving relationship paths, a network, and distinct speech waveforms. Labels remain tied to each reading/listening task. These are conceptual diagrams, not research data.
- Expanded the eight supplied listening accounts to 226–249 words. Each now includes a complication, a competing perspective or a limit to its apparent solution. The accounts remain explicitly fictional; no research findings or authenticated transcripts are claimed.
- Added two **Listen More Closely** prompts per unit: 16 tasks asking learners to distinguish observation from inference, evaluate a decision or consider another interpretation. Suggested reasoning and supporting details appear in the teacher pack; matching prompts appear in the main book and student worksheets.
- Set the scripts in two paragraphs and kept each complete script with its heading on one main-book page.
- Kept Unit 2's nine-question discussion together, removing a flagged 288/84-word spread imbalance without cutting text or shrinking the type. The final visual gate reports no flagged spreads. This does not mean every page has identical density; chapter starts and activity endings retain purposeful white space.

## Protected content

An executed comparison against the baseline confirms that all **24 non-listening chapters' content blocks**, the front matter and all **32 exact chapter titles** are unchanged. The 40 existing listening detail questions/keys are retained. The 74 reading keys and 36 vocabulary completion keys remain supplied. The original manuscript is untouched.

This is a targeted improvement pass, not a claim that an independent word-by-word editorial review has now occurred.
