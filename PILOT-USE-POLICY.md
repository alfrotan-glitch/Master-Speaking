This records permission to prepare and conduct a pilot, not evidence that teaching has started or that learners have completed it. No classroom results are claimed.

## Pilot safeguards

1. Start with the Teacher Implementation Pack's four Unit 1 pilot lessons and the matching Student Practice Pack pages. The teacher should preview each lesson and adjust level, timing and support to the actual class.
2. For listening, use the current original stories with the matching questions in the main book or Student Practice Pack and the Teacher Implementation Pack keys. A teacher or partner reads aloud; no recording is required. Historical video questions and recordings are not inputs to this edition.
3. Keep teacher scripts and answers separate from learners during listening. Open discussion questions accept supported alternatives rather than one prescribed opinion.
4. Limit sharing to the pilot group. Before copying or distributing third-party material, confirm permission or another applicable legal basis; pilot status is not rights clearance. Omit or replace uncleared material where necessary.
5. Allow fictional or neutral alternatives to sensitive personal questions. Recording is optional; obtain consent before recording or sharing learner work. Keep feedback records free of unnecessary personal information.
6. Record actual timings, confusing wording, unsupported or ambiguous answers, accessibility barriers and learner feedback. Pause or adapt any activity that cannot be delivered responsibly. Use the existing teacher-pack observation form; leave unobserved fields blank rather than inventing results.

## Before commercial release

Use R01–R08 in [UNRESOLVED-ISSUES.md](UNRESOLVED-ISSUES.md) as the closure register:

- Use the current supplied stories and matching keys. Historical recordings are excluded, not newly authenticated.
- Review the current authored-artwork provenance and font licenses. Historical imagery and selected quotation prompts are excluded.
- Obtain independent complete proofreading and factual, pronunciation and answer-key review.
- Compare native Word pagination, contents and exports if Word will determine final pagination.
- Complete accessibility and representative reader/device testing; address the untagged PDF.
- Obtain printer-specific specifications, sufficient image resolution, a full cover wrap and physical-proof approval.
- Review actual pilot evidence and resolve material teaching problems.
- Confirm imprint, edition details and ISBN ownership where applicable.

Record the evidence and responsible review for each closure. A reviewer or classroom trial must not be marked complete without actual supporting work. The author/publisher must explicitly approve commercial release after reviewing the closures.
