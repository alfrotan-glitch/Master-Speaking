## Before using the listening lessons

The original external videos are required and are not included. The course instructor must identify and prepare the correct recordings, verify that the questions match, check availability/permissions and arrange captions/transcripts where needed. Thumbnail or lesson-title text is not proof of a video's identity. Do not substitute the other edition's fictional scripts or use its answer keys as verified answers to these videos.

Read **STANDARD-EDITION-REVIEW.md** for the source-preservation evidence, limited copyedits, production checks, visual-review scope, image-quality warnings and outstanding author/publisher decisions.

This is a manuscript-based production/review edition, not an independently approved or commercially cleared publication. Source artwork and quotation permissions remain unresolved. Automated checks are not a substitute for an every-word editorial proof, actual teaching pilot or physical printer proof.

Files named `Master-Speaking-Standard...` are this original-manuscript edition. Existing files without `Standard` are the previously accepted self-contained edition and remain unchanged. Their teacher books and companions have not been revalidated for this original-video edition.
