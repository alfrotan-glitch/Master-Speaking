# Whole-book critical audit and remediation

17 October 2026 • Master Speaking

## Decision

**Substantially improved editorial proof; commercial publication remains on hold.**

This pass examined the eight readings, all 32 chapters’ organization and lesson directions, vocabulary/task references, discussion and presentation scaffolds, pronunciation teaching, output structure, pagination, and navigation. It made concrete corrections rather than issuing another unconditional approval. It is AI-assisted editorial work, not an independent signed human proof, legal opinion, worldwide originality search, or specialist certification.

The reader-facing book contains the course, not this audit or the production discussion. The original `Master Speaking.docx` is unchanged. Existing exact chapter-title wording remains source-backed; no invented editorial chapter names were reintroduced.

## Work completed in this pass

The exact-match chapter/front-matter register now contains **130 text/cell changes**, compared with 50 before this pass: **80 additional guarded changes**. There are also **24 explicit structural repairs** and eight authored review/transfer routines. Counts distinguish copyedits from newly authored learner practice; no new text is misrepresented as recovered manuscript content.

### 1. Corrected significant teaching defects

- **Unit 3 research outline:** removed “Explain the difference between goals and systems,” which had been copied from Unit 2. The body now asks learners to explain emotional intelligence and assess evidence for improving it (printed page(s): 54).
- **Unit 4 pronunciation:** five of the ten primary-stress models were wrong: experience, generosity, responsibility, opportunity and psychological. Both columns now agree on the corrected stress. All ten models are checked against the pinned CMU lexicon (printed page(s): 72).
- **Unit 8 pronunciation:** split six merged weak-form examples into separate readable lines; converted pronunciation subsection labels from numbered questions into real headings; explained why linguistic is a contrast example without schwa (printed page(s): 153).
- **Unit 5 linking:** removed fused respellings such as turnitoff. Underscores now mark links, with an explicit reminder to retain normal spaces in writing. The target is clear connected speech, not speed.
- **Units 1 and 7 pronunciation:** qualified absolute sentence-stress claims and made the voicing test compare sustained consonants, not voiced vowels inside whole words.

### 2. Repaired exercise organization and answerability

- Returned Unit 3 and Unit 7 vocabulary items from pre-reading discussion to Vocabulary Check. Retained Unit 6’s six genuine items rather than deleting one to fit a five-item test assumption.
- Classified the first Unit 1 reading multiple-choice stem correctly so the five questions form a coherent numbered sequence.
- Separated Unit 8’s eight open-response questions from Choose the Best Answer (printed page(s): 142).
- Gave Unit 1 listening explicit multiple-choice and true/false directions; repaired A/B subsection labels in Units 1 and 7. Unit 5 reading now has a true/false instruction. Listening answers themselves remain unverified.
- Repaired vocabulary blanks that could not naturally take their supplied phrases; clarified grammatical form changes where needed.
- Fixed definitions that confused a strong impression with a positive impression, balance with equality, equal distribution with fairness, and debt with overspending.

### 3. Made directions complete and learner-facing

- Completed the broken Unit 4 debate direction ending “Give reasons and Student A”; matched it to the two printed student roles.
- Removed duplicate directions in Units 3, 4, 6 and 8. Unit 8 now has one research instruction and one topic-choice direction rather than five overlapping directions.
- Replaced undefined Day 2/Day 3 and Reading Toolbox references with actual chapter/section references.
- Removed remaining teacher-facing asides in the reading and linking practice. Instructions now address the learner.
- Replaced generic, unverified TED-Ed attribution in task directions with neutral instructions to study expressions before listening. This does not remove the requirement to identify and credit the actual recordings.

### 4. Strengthened reading and critical thinking without invented evidence

- Replaced vague appeals to “language experts” and unidentified goal-writing research with practical, transparent learner advice.
- Qualified the instruction never to abandon a goal; learners may adapt a plan or reconsider a goal when circumstances change.
- Removed a sweeping historical/predictive IQ comparison; attributed the five-part emotional-intelligence framework as a model, not the only universal definition.
- Marked the Unit 8 future-language scenario as a hypothetical assumption, not a demographic forecast.
- Added explicit privacy and respectful-boundary guidance in the relationships unit. Compromise is not framed as accepting threats, coercion or violence; fictional examples remain acceptable.

### 5. Completed the learning cycle

- Completed missing presentation conclusions in Units 6 and 8 and supplied guidance for empty conclusion prompts in Units 4, 5 and 7.
- Added a Sources Used prompt in every research chapter. This asks students to identify their own evidence; it is **not** an authored bibliography validating the book’s research claims.
- Added eight unit-specific Reflect and Try Again tasks: listener repair; a realistic practice habit; perspective-taking; a budget trade-off; checking an AI claim; respectful boundaries; investigating an international connection; and explaining a local idea to a multilingual audience.
- Each routine connects peer feedback to revision and a practical application, rather than simply adding more questions. These tasks have not yet been classroom-piloted.

### 6. Rechecked design and navigation after repagination

- Fixed an exposed layout defect where a reading title and illustration could appear without the opening passage on the same page. Word stores the keep-with-next property on reading figures, the PDF renderer honors it, and the lesson gate checks all eight actual reading openings.
- All 32 chapters still start on separate PDF pages; all eight unit openers remain recto. Contents folios/links, bookmarks, reader offsets, and exact-title navigation were recalculated and checked.
- Official EPUBCheck passes. No new claims of PDF tagging, accessible-device behavior, native Word pagination, or print approval are inferred from that pass.

## Pronunciation reference and its limits

Reference: [CMU Pronouncing Dictionary](https://github.com/cmusphinx/cmudict), revision `74790861f652b15e4ac49015a90074ad62a27690`, file `cmudict.dict`. The ten selected ARPAbet entries and primary-stress positions are recorded in `book/publishing/pronunciation-reference.json`, with the source-file hash. The original license is retained at `book/assets/cmudict-LICENSE.txt`.

The comparison verifies the intended primary-stress syllable in that US English lexicon. It does **not** certify all dialects, full IPA transcriptions, intonation choices, phonetic teaching technique, or a whole-course accent standard. Spelling-based chunks in the learner table are explicitly labeled visual stress guides rather than phonetic transcriptions. No lexicon endorsement of this book is claimed.
