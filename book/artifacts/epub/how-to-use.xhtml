How to Use This Book

A Practical Guide for Students

Welcome to the TOEFL House-Master speaking course designed to help you do more than simply learn English.

This book is designed to help you use English to think, discuss, question, explain, argue, and communicate ideas.

The topics become increasingly challenging, but the goal is not to give you perfect answers. The goal is to help you become a more confident, independent, and thoughtful English speaker.

How Is Each Unit Organized?

Each unit is divided into four chapters.

Chapter 1 — Speaking through Reading

You will be introduced to the week’s topic through questions, ideas, and activities.

  • activate your background knowledge
  • learn useful topic-related language
  • discuss general questions
  • share your personal experiences
  • express and defend your opinions

Chapter 2 — Speaking through Listening

You will work with an authentic video or speech connected to the topic.

  • watch and listen for meaning
  • learn vocabulary from authentic English
  • complete comprehension activities
  • analyze the speaker’s ideas
  • discuss controversial or thought-provoking questions

Chapter 3 — Speaking through Discussion

This is your main communication day.

  • collocations and phrasal verbs
  • discussion expressions
  • vocabulary activation
  • group discussions
  • ranking activities
  • challenge questions
  • spontaneous speaking

Chapter 4 — Speaking through Research Presentation

You will move from discussion to academic speaking.

  • practice a pronunciation skill
  • learn presentation expressions
  • research a question
  • organize your ideas
  • use evidence and examples
  • prepare a short presentation
  • respond to questions from your classmates
  • By the end of the unit, you should be able to speak about the topic in a structured and academically appropriate way.
