Here is a list of most useful expressions to learn.

ExpressionMeaningExample
pay attentionto listen or watch carefullyGood listeners always pay attention to the speaker.
make someone feel understoodto help someone feel that you truly understand themA good friend makes you feel understood when you share your problems.
go through the motionsto do something without real interest or effortDon’t just go through the motions when someone is talking to you.
remove distractionsto eliminate anything that takes your attention awayBefore the meeting, everyone removed distractions by putting away their phones.
interrupt someoneto stop someone while they are speakingIt is rude to interrupt someone before they finish speaking.
ask open-ended questionsto ask questions that require more than “yes” or “no” answersA good teacher asks open-ended questions to encourage discussion.
stay presentto keep your full attention on what is happening nowTry to stay present during conversations instead of thinking about your reply.
ask for clarificationto ask someone to explain something more clearlyIf you don’t understand, ask for clarification politely.
keep an open mindto be willing to consider different ideas or opinionsGood listeners keep an open mind even when they disagree.
change someone’s mindto persuade someone to think differentlyListening respectfully is more effective than trying to force someone to change their mind.
