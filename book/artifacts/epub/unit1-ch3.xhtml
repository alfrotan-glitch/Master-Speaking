Vocabulary Activation

Work in pairs. Choose 10 expressions and create a short conversation using all ten.

Guided Discussion

Now students already have the language they need.

Discussion 1

Communication at School

  1. Why do some students hesitate to speak in class?
  2. How can teachers encourage students to communicate more?
  3. What makes classroom discussions successful?
  4. Is making mistakes necessary for learning? Why?

Discussion 2

Communication in Daily Life

  1. What communication skills are important in a family?
  2. Have you ever experienced a misunderstanding? How did you solve it?
  3. How can people become better listeners?
  4. Why do some conversations fail?

Discussion 3

Communication and Success

  1. Can communication change a person’s future?
  2. Which is more important: knowledge or communication?
  3. How does communication affect leadership?
  4. What advice would you give shy English learners?

Speaking Challenge

One student speaks for two minutes answering this question:

  1. What is the most important communication skill, and why?

Requirements:

Use five collocations or expressions from today’s toolbox.

Give two reasons.
