Pronunciation Clinic

Skill: Sentence Stress

Good speakers do not stress every word. They stress important words (content words) and reduce grammar words (function words). This helps listeners understand the main message.

Compare

I LIKE TO SPEAK ENGLISH WITH MY FRIENDS EVERY DAY.

(Natural? No.)

I like to SPEAK ENGLISH with my FRIENDS every DAY.

Practice

Read these sentences aloud. Stress the bold words.

Communication is an essential life skill.

Good listeners ask follow-up questions.

Practice builds confidence.

Great speakers always respect their audience.

Clear communication helps people solve problems.

Essential Presentation Expressions

Learn these expressions before preparing your presentation.

ExpressionPurpose
Good morning everyone.Opening
Today I would like to talk about...Introducing the topic
The purpose of my presentation is...Stating the objective
First, let’s look at...First point

As you can see...Referring to visuals or ideas

Rules

Speak for 3–4 minutes.

Do not read your paper word for word.

Maintain eye contact.

Speak clearly.

Use at least five expressions from the Presentation Toolbox.

Use at least five collocations from Day 2 or Day 3.
