Listening Strategy

First Viewing

✔ Do not write.

✔ Listen for the main idea.

Ask yourself:

  1. What is the speaker trying to convince us to do?
Second Viewing

Take notes.

Focus on key ideas, examples, reasons. Do not try to write every word.

Listening Comprehension

A. Multiple Choice

Choose the best answer.

  1. What was Emmanuel Acho’s main goal in college?
    1. a. To become a sports psychologist
    2. b. To be drafted in the first three rounds of the NFL Draft
    3. c. To become a football coach
  2. What happened during the NFL Combine?
    1. a. He tore his quad while running.
    2. b. He won the competition.
    3. c. He received a scholarship.
  3. According to the speaker, why do many people set goals?
    1. a. Because they have been taught that goals are necessary for success.
    2. b. Because goals are always easy to achieve.
    3. c. Because goals eliminate failure.
  4. What does Emmanuel suggest instead of setting goals?
    1. a. Avoid making plans.
    2. b. Have an objective without limitations.
    3. c. Change your goals every month.
  5. What is the speaker’s final message?
    1. a. Goals create limitless opportunities.
    2. b. Believe in possibility rather than being limited by goals.
    3. c. Success depends only on talent.

B. True or False

Write T (True) or F (False). Correct the false statements.

  • _____ Emmanuel wanted to leave college early and enter the NFL Draft.
  • _____ Emmanuel achieved his goal of being drafted in the first three rounds.
  • _____ The speaker believes writing goals down automatically guarantees success.
  • _____ Emmanuel says people often set goals because they want feedback.
  • _____ The speaker believes possibilities are greater than goals.

Group Discussion

Work in groups of 3–4 students. Discuss the following questions.

  1. Do you think setting goals is enough to become successful? Why or why not?
  2. Have you ever worked tirelessly to achieve something? What was the result?
  3. What usually prevents people from reaching their full potential?
  4. Do you agree that some people are held captive by fear or failure? Give an example.
  5. What is one goal you have this year? What will you do to go the extra mile and achieve it?
  6. Is it better to focus on the final result or on your daily habits? Explain your opinion.
  7. Can trying too hard sometimes cause harm to your health or personal life? Why?
  8. What is your biggest objective in learning English? How will you achieve it?
  9. Think of a successful person you admire. Which expressions from today’s lesson describe that person best?
  10. Challenge Question

The speaker says we should believe in possibility instead of limiting ourselves with goals.

  1. Do you agree or disagree? Explain your opinion with reasons and examples.
