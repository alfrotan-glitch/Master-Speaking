Speaking Toolbox

Study 10 carefully selected collocations, phrasal verbs, and discussion expressions related to the week’s topic.

ExpressionMeaningExample
set high standards for yourselfto expect a high level of performance from yourselfSuccessful people often set high standards for themselves.

lose sight of your prioritiesto forget what is most importantMany students lose sight of their priorities because of social media.

push yourself beyond your limitsto challenge yourself to do more than you think you canAthletes often push themselves beyond their limits.

Vocabulary Activation

Work in pairs. Choose 10 expressions and create a short conversation using all ten.

Guided Discussion

Work in groups of 3–4 students. Use at least eight expressions from the Speaking Toolbox.

Discussion Questions

  1. Why do some people achieve their goals while others fail?
  2. Which is more important: talent or hard work? Why?
  3. What bad habits stop students from succeeding?
  4. Is failure necessary for success? Explain.
  5. How can students stay motivated during difficult times?
  6. Should parents set goals for their children, or should children choose their own goals?
  7. What daily habits lead to long-term success?
  8. If you could change one habit in your life today, what would it be?

Mini Debate

Motion

Success depends more on habits than goals.

Team A: Agree

Team B: Disagree

Each student should speak for one minute.
