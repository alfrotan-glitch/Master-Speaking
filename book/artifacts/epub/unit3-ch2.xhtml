Listening Comprehension

A. Multiple Choice

Choose the best answer.

  1. Why are the two students disappointed at the beginning of the video?
    1. a. They forgot to take the exam.
    2. b. They received lower grades than they expected.
    3. c. They had to attend summer classes.
  2. According to the Process Model, what happens immediately after we enter a situation?
    1. a. We change our behavior.
    2. b. The situation draws our attention.
    3. c. We experience an emotional response.
  3. Which strategy does the speaker suggest if you attend a party where your ex is present?
    1. a. Argue with your ex.
    2. b. Leave immediately.
    3. c. Avoid interacting with your ex.
  4. Which of the following is considered a healthy way to regulate emotions?
    1. a. Hiding your emotions.
    2. b. Taking recreational drugs.
    3. c. Talking to someone you trust.
  5. What is the speaker’s main message?
    1. a. People should always try to stay happy.
    2. b. Every emotion can be useful depending on the situation.
    3. c. Negative emotions should always be avoided.

B. True or False

Write T (True) or F (False). Correct the false statements.

  • _____ The Process Model explains five steps in the formation of emotions.
  • _____ Reappraisal means changing the way you think about a situation.
  • _____ The speaker recommends hiding your emotions whenever possible.
  • _____ Feeling sad can sometimes help us support other people.
  • _____ Research suggests that constantly chasing happiness may create additional negative emotions.

Group Discussion

Discuss the questions in groups of three or four. Give everyone a turn and ask for clarification when a point is unclear.

  1. What is the Process Model?
  2. According to the speaker, why is learning to notice your emotions important?
  3. What are two healthy strategies for regulating emotions after an emotional response?
  4. Why can sadness sometimes be a helpful emotion?
  5. In your opinion, which strategy from the video would be most useful in your daily life? Why?

Think Beyond

  1. Do you agree that there are no “good” or “bad” emotions, only helpful or unhelpful ones? Why or why not?
  2. Should schools teach students how to regulate their emotions as part of the curriculum? Give reasons.
  3. In your opinion, is emotional intelligence more important than academic intelligence in today’s workplace? Explain.
  4. Some people believe we should always stay positive. Others believe we should accept every emotion we feel. Which view do you agree with? Why?
