Think – Pair – Share

Discuss the following questions with your partner.

  1. If someone offered you $1 million, but you could never see your family again, would you accept? Why or why not?
  2. Which do you think has a greater impact on happiness: earning more money or having meaningful relationships?
  3. Do you think there should be a limit to how much money people try to earn? Explain your answer.

Listening Comprehension

A. Multiple Choice

Choose the best answer.

  1. What is the speaker’s main argument?
    1. a. Money cannot improve happiness under any circumstances.
    2. b. People become happier when they earn more money.
    3. c. People can increase their happiness by spending money on others.
  2. According to the speaker, what often happens to lottery winners?
    1. a. They become happier and build stronger friendships.
    2. b. They often go into debt and experience damaged relationships.
    3. c. They donate most of their money to charity.
  3. In the University of British Columbia experiment, participants were asked to
    1. a. save all of their money.
    2. b. spend money either on themselves or on someone else.
    3. c. invest their money in a business.
  4. What did the researchers discover about the amount of money?
    1. a. Spending larger amounts always created greater happiness.
    2. b. Five dollars created more happiness than twenty dollars.
    3. c. How the money was spent mattered more than how much was spent.
  5. According to the Gallup survey, people who donated money to charity generally
    1. a. reported higher levels of happiness.
    2. b. earned higher salaries.
    3. c. spent less money on themselves.

B. True or False

Write T (True) or F (False). Correct the false statements.

  • _____ The speaker believes the saying “Money can’t buy happiness” is completely correct.
  • _____ Lottery winners often experience financial and social problems.
  • _____ Participants who spent money on themselves became much happier than those who spent it on others.
  • _____ The research in Uganda produced results similar to those in Canada.
  • _____ The speaker concludes that generosity can improve both happiness and teamwork.

C. Short Answer

Answer the questions in complete sentences.

  1. Why does the speaker believe many people fail to use money in a way that increases happiness?
  2. What happened when participants spent money on other people instead of themselves?
  3. Why did the researchers conduct a similar experiment in Uganda?
  4. What did the Gallup Organization’s international data reveal about charitable giving and happiness?
  5. According to the speaker, what is the most effective way to use money if you want to become happier?

Group Discussion

Work in groups of 3–4 students. Discuss the following questions.

  1. Do you agree with the speaker that money can buy happiness if it is spent on other people? Why or why not?
  2. Have you ever spent money on someone else and felt happier afterward? Describe your experience.
  3. The speaker says many lottery winners go into debt and damage their relationships. Why do you think this happens?
  4. In your opinion, which brings more happiness: buying something for yourself or giving a meaningful gift to someone else? Explain.
  5. The speaker suggests that pro-social behavior increases happiness. Besides giving money, what other pro-social actions can people take?

The research found that the amount of money doesn’t matter as much as how it is spent. Do you agree? Give reasons and examples.

  1. Have you ever pooled your money with friends or classmates to buy something for another person? If not, would you like to? Why?
  2. Do you think the speaker’s findings hold true in your country? Why or why not?

Some people believe that earning more money should be their highest priority, while others believe that helping others leads to a happier life. Which view do you support? Explain.

Challenge Question

Imagine you receive $100 that you must spend today. You have two choices:

Spend all of it on yourself.

Spend all of it helping someone else.

  1. Which option would you choose, and why? Do you think your choice would make you happier? Support your answer with examples.
