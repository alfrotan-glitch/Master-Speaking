Vocabulary Activation

Complete each sentence using one expression from the Speaking Toolbox.

  • Instead of buying unnecessary things, I try to __________________________ every month.
  • University students should __________________________ by learning useful skills.
  • Having a stable job can __________________________ because you worry less about unexpected expenses.
  • If people spend more than they earn, they fail to __________________________.
  • Some people __________________________ the size of their bank account, while others value relationships more.

Guided Discussion

Discuss in groups of three or four. Use at least eight toolbox expressions across the discussion, making sure they fit the point you are making.

  1. What does financial freedom mean to you?
  2. Do you usually put money aside or spend it immediately? Why?
  3. Have you ever had a priceless experience that money could not replace? Describe it.
  4. If you suddenly received a large amount of money, what would you do first?
  5. In your opinion, how much money does a person need to live comfortably?
  6. Do you believe that money talks in today’s society? Give examples.
  7. Is it more important to make a living or to enjoy your work? Explain.
  8. Who should teach children how to manage money: families, schools, or both? Why?
  9. Is financial freedom more important than having a high salary? Why?
  10. How might financial pressures differ for people with high and low incomes? Give an example without assuming everyone in either group has the same experience.

Mini Debate

Work in pairs. Student A defends the first statement, and Student B defends the second. Give reasons and examples, then respond to your partner’s argument.

Student A

“Money is the most important factor in a happy life.”

Student B

“Relationships, health, and purpose are more important than money.”

Instructions

Prepare three arguments, each with a reason and supporting example or evidence.

Use at least five expressions from today’s Speaking Toolbox.

Support your opinion with one real-life example and explain the connection.

Ask your partner at least two follow-up questions.

Try to persuade your partner respectfully.
