Complete each sentence using one expression from the Speaking Toolbox.

  • University students should __________________________ by learning useful skills.
  • Having a stable job can __________________________ because you worry less about unexpected expenses.
  • If people spend more than they earn, they fail to __________________________.
  • Some people __________________________ the size of their bank account, while others value relationships more.

Guided Discussion

Work in groups of 3–4 students. Use at least eight expressions from the Speaking Toolbox.

Discuss the following questions with your group. Use the expressions from the Speaking Toolbox whenever possible.

  1. What does financial freedom mean to you?
  2. Do you usually put money aside or spend it immediately? Why?
  3. Have you ever had a priceless experience that money could not replace? Describe it.
  4. If you suddenly received a large amount of money, what would you do first?
  5. In your opinion, how much money does a person need to live comfortably?
  6. Do you believe that money talks in today’s society? Give examples.
  7. Is it more important to make a living or to enjoy your work? Explain.
  8. Should parents teach children how to manage money at school or at home?
  9. Is financial freedom more important than having a high salary? Why?
  10. Do you think wealthy people are under more pressure than ordinary people? Explain.
