Research Task

Research your topic using reliable sources.

  1. What do psychologists say about money and happiness?
  2. What is the difference between wealth and well-being?
  3. Why do some wealthy people still feel unhappy?
  4. How can generosity improve happiness?
  5. Besides money, what factors contribute to a happy life?
  6. What does your country’s culture teach about money and happiness?

Reflect and Try Again

Ask a partner: Was my main point clear? Did my evidence support it? Where did you need clarification? Revise one passage and say it again.

Check your progress: Can you explain a choice about money and well-being, acknowledge a trade-off, and distinguish your values from a research claim?

Put it to use: Compare two uses of a fictional budget. Explain one benefit and one trade-off for each, then consider whose needs your plan may overlook. Revise your recommendation after a partner’s question or one you write yourself. Keep a progress note: your original choice, the question, and why you changed or kept your recommendation.
