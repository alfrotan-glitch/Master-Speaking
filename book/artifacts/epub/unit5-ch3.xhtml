Vocabulary Activation

With a partner, choose ten toolbox expressions and plan a short conversation using all ten. Give each speaker a reason to respond, not just a list of phrases.

Guided Discussion

Discuss in groups of three or four. Use at least eight toolbox expressions across the discussion, making sure they fit the point you are making.

Discussion Questions

  1. Which jobs are most likely to be affected by AI?
  2. Do you think AI will create as many jobs as it eliminates? Why?
  3. What skills will become more valuable when AI performs more routine tasks?
  4. Should companies be responsible for retraining workers whose jobs are replaced by AI?
  5. If your profession changed dramatically because of AI, how would you adapt?

Mini Debate

Job Creator or Job Destroyer?

Divide the class into two groups.

Group A

AI will ultimately create more opportunities than it destroys.

Prepare arguments about:

  • new professions
  • increased productivity
  • shorter working hours
  • new industries
  • human-AI collaboration

Group B

AI will ultimately destroy more opportunities than it creates.

Prepare arguments about:

  • unemployment
  • automation
  • inequality
  • loss of human skills
  • dependence on technology

Debate Rules

Each group must:

Present three main arguments and explain the reason supporting each.

Give at least one identifiable real-world example; explain what it shows and what it does not establish.

Respond to an argument from the opposing group.

Use at least five toolbox expressions accurately as you develop and respond to arguments.

Speaking Challenge

Give a one-minute prediction.
