Pronunciation Clinic

Skill: Linking Sounds

When native speakers speak naturally, the words in a sentence often connect, rather than being pronounced as separate units.

For example:

  • turn_it_off → turnitoff
  • pick_it_up → pickitup
  • work_in → workin
  • depend_on → dependon

This is especially useful for your students because it helps them sound more fluent and natural, particularly during presentations and extended speaking.

Practice from today’s AI topic

Have students practice these phrases first slowly, then naturally:

depend on AI

work with AI

adapt to AI

rely on technology

make informed decisions

keep up with technology

hand over control

use AI responsibly

deal with the consequences

work alongside AI

Sentence Practice

Students read each sentence three times:

  • 1. Slowly → separate words
  • 2. Naturally → connect the sounds
  • 3. Fluently → focus on meaning, not individual words

We need to adapt to AI.

People rely on technology every day.

We shouldn’t hand over control to machines.

Students need to learn how to use AI responsibly.

Humans will have to work alongside AI.

Clinic tip:

Don’t pronounce every word separately. Connect words that naturally belong together.

The goal isn’t to make students speak faster. The goal is to make their speech smoother.

Research Task

Research your topic using reliable sources.

Choose one of the following topics.

Topic 1 – AI and Education

  1. How is AI changing the way students learn?
  2. What are the benefits of using AI in education?
  3. What problems can AI create for students?
  4. Should students be allowed to use AI for assignments?
  5. How can teachers encourage responsible AI use?

Topic 2 – AI and Employment

  1. Which jobs are most likely to be affected by AI?
  2. What is technological unemployment?
  3. Will AI create new jobs as it replaces existing ones?
  4. What skills will workers need in the future?
  5. How can governments and companies help workers adapt?

Topic 3 – AI and Human Dependence

  1. Why are people becoming increasingly dependent on technology?
  2. What could happen if humans rely too heavily on AI?
  3. Could AI weaken people’s ability to think independently?
  4. How can we maintain human skills while using AI?
  5. Where should we draw the line between assistance and dependence?

Topic 4 – AI and Ethics

  1. Who should be responsible when an AI system causes harm?
  2. What ethical concerns are associated with powerful AI?
  3. Should humans always remain in control of important decisions?
  4. How can AI developers reduce unintended consequences?
  5. Why is human oversight important?

Topic 5 – AI and the Future

  1. What might general-purpose AI be capable of in the future?
  2. How could AI change everyday life?
  3. What benefits could advanced AI bring?
  4. What risks could advanced AI create?
  5. How should society prepare for the future of AI?
