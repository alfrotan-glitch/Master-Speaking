Think

Think about the questions before discussing them. Note a few key words, then explain one answer to a partner. You may use a fictional example or pass on a personal question.

Some people believe AI will improve our lives, while others believe it is dangerous. Which opinion do you agree with? Why?
Would you trust AI to make important decisions about your health, education, or finances? Explain your answer.
Do you think future jobs will require people to know how to work with AI? Why or why not?
