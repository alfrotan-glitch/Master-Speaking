Listening Strategy

First Viewing

✔ Do not write.

✔ Listen for the main idea.

Ask yourself:

  1. What is the speaker trying to convince us to do?
Second Viewing

Take notes.

Focus on key ideas, examples, reasons. Do not try to write every word.

Listening Comprehension

A. Multiple Choice

Choose the best answer.

  1. What is the speaker’s main purpose?
    1. a. To explain why people should avoid marriage
    2. b. To present six ingredients that can help relationships thrive
    3. c. To argue that sexual chemistry is the most important part of love
  2. How does the speaker distinguish attraction from sexual chemistry?
    1. a. Attraction lasts longer and involves wanting to continue discovering the other person.
    2. b. Attraction is completely biological, while sexual chemistry is emotional.
    3. c. They are essentially the same thing.
  3. According to the speaker, what helps build trust?
    1. a. Grand and expensive apologies
    2. b. Small promises that are consistently kept
    3. c. Avoiding difficult conversations
  4. What does compassion mean in the speaker’s explanation?
    1. a. Experiencing exactly the same emotions as your partner
    2. b. Feeling for your partner while maintaining your own emotional stability
    3. c. Ignoring your partner’s emotions to protect yourself
  5. What does a shared vision help couples do?
    1. a. Avoid making compromises completely
    2. b. Decide where they want to go and prioritize their resources accordingly
    3. c. Make sure they have identical personalities

B. True or False

Write T (True) or F (False). Correct the false statements.

The speaker’s research involved 450 couples.

  • _____ The couples in the study had all been together for approximately the same amount of time.
  • _____ The speaker believes attraction is limited to sexual chemistry.
  • _____ Respect involves not taking your partner for granted.
  • _____ Trust is built through consistent and reliable behavior.
  • _____ The speaker says couples should always sacrifice for each other.
  • _____ A shared vision involves knowing where you want to go as individuals and as a couple.
  • _____ The speaker argues that loving behaviors are simply automatic once people fall in love.

C. Short Answer

Answer the questions in complete sentences.

  1. Can respect exist without love?
  2. Is it possible to lose attraction to someone but still have a strong relationship?
  3. What everyday behaviors show that someone respects their partner?

Group Discussion

Work in groups of 3–4 students. Discuss the following questions.

  1. Why are small promises important for building trust?
  2. Is trust easy to rebuild after it has been broken?
  3. What is the difference between empathy and compassion?
  4. Can being too emotionally involved in your partner’s problems become unhealthy?
  5. What does it really mean to “show up” for someone?
  6. Should married couples have the same long-term goals?
  7. What happens when two partners have completely different ideas about their future?
  8. Is compromise healthier than sacrifice? Why?
  9. Do loving behaviors become less important after people have been together for many years?
