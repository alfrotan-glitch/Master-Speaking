Pronunciation Clinic

Skill: Linking Sounds

The “-ed” Ending: /t/, /d/, or /ɪd/?

This is a very useful pronunciation problem for B2+ students, especially when they are speaking about relationships, experiences, and research.

The -ed ending has three possible pronunciations:

1. /t/

After a voiceless sound such as /k/, /p/, /s/, /ʃ/, /tʃ/, and /f/.

liked → /laɪkt/

kissed → /kɪst/

discussed → /dɪˈskʌst/

helped → /helpt/

They discussed their problems.

2. /d/

After a voiced sound such as /b/, /g/, /v/, /z/, /m/, /n/, /l/, and vowels.

loved → /lʌvd/

shared → /ʃerd/

learned → /lɜːrnd/

showed → /ʃoʊd/

They shared a vision for their future.

3. /ɪd/

Only after /t/ or /d/ sounds.

wanted → /ˈwɒntɪd/

needed → /ˈniːdɪd/

respected → /rɪˈspektɪd/

committed → /kəˈmɪtɪd/

They respected each other’s boundaries.

Quick Practice

Ask students to put these words into three groups:

loved — respected — trusted — liked — shared — wanted — discussed — needed — helped — showed — kissed — committed

/t//d//ɪd/

Speaking Challenge 2

Students choose one word from each column and create a sentence about relationships.

For example:

They liked each other, shared similar values, and respected each other’s boundaries.

Research Task

Research your topic using reliable sources.

Choose one of the following topics.

Choose ONE of the following questions:

1. Is Love Enough for a Successful Marriage?

  1. What do psychologists say about love and long-term relationships?
  2. What other factors contribute to marital satisfaction?
  3. Why might love alone not be enough?

2. Communication and Conflict in Relationships

  1. Why do couples experience conflict?
  2. What communication strategies can help couples resolve disagreements?
  3. What behaviors can make conflict worse?

3. What Makes a Marriage Last?

  1. What factors are commonly associated with successful marriages?
  2. What behaviors help couples maintain closeness?
  3. What can cause relationships to break down?

4. Marriage and Financial Stress

  1. How can financial problems affect relationships?
  2. Should married couples combine their finances?
  3. How can couples reduce financial conflict?

5. Marriage in the Modern World

  1. How has marriage changed compared with previous generations?
  2. How have technology and social media affected relationships?
  3. What challenges do modern couples face?

6. Love across Cultures

  1. How do different cultures view marriage?
  2. What role do family expectations play?
  3. How can cultural differences affect relationships?
