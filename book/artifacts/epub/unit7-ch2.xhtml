Listening Comprehension

A. Multiple Choice

Choose the best answer.

  1. Why does Goldin say the future is unpredictable?
    1. A. People are not interested in the future.
    2. B. Even experts and major institutions frequently make incorrect predictions.
    3. C. Technology has stopped developing.
  2. What does Goldin identify as two “Achilles heels” of globalization?
    1. A. Technology and migration
    2. B. Growing inequality and complexity
    3. C. Poverty and unemployment
  3. What does Goldin mean by “systemic risk”?
    1. A. A problem that affects only one individual
    2. B. A problem that can spread through interconnected systems
    3. C. A risk caused only by technology
  4. What does Goldin predict about computing by 2030?
    1. A. Computers will become less powerful.
    2. B. Computing power will improve enormously for the same price.
    3. C. Computers will disappear.
  5. What concern does Goldin raise about genetic technology?
    1. A. It may become available only to wealthy people.
    2. B. It will completely stop medical development.
    3. C. It will make people live shorter lives.
  6. Why does Goldin believe migration may become increasingly important?
    1. A. Countries will need people to support their economies and aging populations.
    2. B. People will no longer want to live in their own countries.
    3. C. Technology will eliminate all national borders.
  7. Which of the following does Goldin mention as a systemic risk?
    1. A. Fashion
    2. B. Climate change
    3. C. Tourism
  8. What does Goldin believe humanity needs to do?
    1. A. Stop technological development.
    2. B. Allow only wealthy countries to develop technology.
    3. C. Develop new ways of collectively managing global challenges.
