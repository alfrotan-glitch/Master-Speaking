Speaking Toolbox

Globalization: Opportunity or Threat?

Study 10 carefully selected collocations, phrasal verbs, and discussion expressions related to the week’s topic.

Vocabulary / ExpressionMeaningExample
take a positionto choose and express a particular opinion about an issueBefore the debate, students had to take a position on globalization.
to some extentpartly, but not completelyI agree to some extent, but globalization also creates serious problems.
look at something differentlyhave a different way of understanding or thinking about somethingI understand your argument, but I look at the issue differently.
support an argumentto provide reasons or evidence that make an argument strongerYou need to use examples to support your argument.
create opportunitiesto make new possibilities for success or development availableGlobalization can create opportunities for people in developing countries.
widen the gapto make the difference between two groups largerUnequal access to technology can widen the gap between rich and poor.
benefit fromto gain an advantage or positive result from somethingNot everyone benefits from globalization equally.
pose a threat toto create a possible danger or problem for somethingGlobal competition can pose a threat to small local businesses.
address a problemto take action to deal with a problemGovernments need to address the problem of economic inequality.
take responsibility forto accept the duty of dealing with somethingCountries should take responsibility for reducing environmental damage.

Vocabulary Activation

Work in pairs. Choose 10 expressions and create a short conversation using all ten.

Guided Discussion

Work in groups of 3–4 students. Use at least eight expressions from the Speaking Toolbox.

Discussion Questions

It is the year 2050. You are members of an international committee responsible for designing a better global future.

Your committee has a limited budget and can focus on only three areas.

Choose three:

Climate change

Artificial intelligence

Global education

Economic inequality

Genetic technology

Migration

Global healthcare

Environmental protection

Employment

International peace

  1. Which three should receive the most attention?
  2. Why are they more important than the others?
  3. What should governments do?
  4. What should individuals do?

What Does This Mean?

Then discuss:

  1. How can globalization be its own strength and weakness?
  2. What is the biggest benefit of globalization?
  3. What is its biggest danger?
  4. Can we enjoy the benefits without creating the risks?
  5. Who should be responsible for managing those risks?

Speaking Challenge

After everything you have discussed in this unit, what is your answer to the question:

“What is one prediction about the future that you strongly agree with, and one that you disagree with?”

Give a 1-minute response and support your opinion with at least two reasons and one example.
