Vocabulary Activation

With a partner, choose ten toolbox expressions and plan a short conversation using all ten. Give each speaker a reason to respond, not just a list of phrases.

Guided Discussion

Discuss in groups of three or four. Use at least eight toolbox expressions across the discussion, making sure they fit the point you are making.

Discussion Questions

  1. Why has English become such an effective lingua franca?
  2. Do you think English really helps people communicate across cultures?
  3. How can English help bridge a language gap, and what cultural understanding might still be needed?
  4. Is it more important to speak English accurately or to express an idea clearly?
  5. How can people become better at adapting to different accents?
  6. Does being exposed to English automatically make someone a better speaker?
  7. What is the best way to overcome a language barrier?
  8. Should international universities place a premium on English proficiency?

English and Academic Opportunity

  1. Can someone have excellent academic knowledge but poor English?
  2. Why do people sometimes equate English ability with intelligence?
  3. Should a student’s academic ability be judged separately from their English ability?
  4. Should international students be judged by their English when applying to university?
  5. Do English tests sometimes create opportunities, or can they also create barriers?
  6. Should every university student be required to reach a certain level of English?

Speaking Challenge

  1. If a brilliant scientist has weak English but has discovered something that could save millions of lives, should an English-language university be allowed to reject that scientist because of their English?

Defend your position.
