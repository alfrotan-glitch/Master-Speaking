# Master Speaking — authored classroom edition

**Date:** 23 October 2026

## Educational aim

A speaking course should change what a learner can **do with another person**. This edition treats a lesson as a complete interaction cycle: understand a problem; choose meaningful language; speak without reading a script; listen and respond; repair what was unclear; reflect; and transfer the skill to a different situation. Long or complex wording has no value unless it communicates a useful meaning.

## Authored changes

- Eight integrated Student Book Speaking Studios, one per original unit, with distinct situations, role cards, challenge discussions, model conversations, one-minute presentations, peer review and real-world transfer.
- A matching eight-unit Master Speaking Teacher's Book with lesson outcomes, pre-teaching notes, sixty-minute implementation plans, common mistakes, feedback, optional support and unscripted extension, plus a final performance record and explicit four-criterion descriptors.
- Seven original offline listening passages (Units 2–8) with four comprehension questions and verified author-written answer keys, plus the existing original Unit 1 active-listening passage and matched key. These passages do **not** pretend to be copies or transcripts of the linked third-party recordings.
- A cross-book optional grammar bridge covering all sixteen lessons of Grammar for Special Speaking. Grammar remains a tool for communication, not a separate student pathway.
- The outdated fixed-page printed contents is replaced with truthful thematic navigation; the PDF/EPUB retain curated topic navigation.
- Student and teacher deliverables regenerate from one shared original lesson source, and the release tests confirm all eight tasks, their placement, original-manuscript retention, publication metadata, links and A4 packaging.

## Classroom method

A single shared class moves from one first attempt to feedback and a second attempt. The teacher may offer an example sentence or a planning card, then withdraw support on the next attempt. Every learner should be able to contribute a meaningful response; no learner is labelled or assigned a different book. All personal/sensitive scenarios can be fictionalized.

**Assessment:** four equally weighted criteria—message and reasoning, listening and interaction, language choices, delivery and repair. The final capstone is a ninety-second argument and two unscripted questions. Never score accent identity or memorized grammatical complexity as a proxy for communication.

## What final publication still requires

The automated checks are evidence for structural correctness only. The source manuscript remains much longer than the new eight studios; a full independent line edit and proofread are still necessary. Two Word drawing objects still need visual inspection for genuinely appropriate alt-text/decorative treatment. Third-party image and recording permissions require independent verification. Official EPUBCheck 5.4.0 checks the EPUB format in CI; actual reader-device testing, all-page rendered proofing and real-classroom piloting still require separate evidence. Do not publish or sell the books as definitively error-free until these are signed off.
