Items can sample more than one target; this matrix identifies the principal target being scored. It is a blueprint, not evidence of validated difficulty or reliability.

## Interpretation and limits

- The diagnostic grammar test (25 items) is an initial snapshot, not a parallel form of the 30-item final test. **Do not compare raw totals or describe their difference as a learning gain.**
- The baseline and final speaking samples use the same prompts, time conditions, interaction instructions, and four-criterion rubric. Their criterion scores can support a classroom comparison when the same scoring guidance is applied. They have not been validated for inter-rater reliability.
- Item coverage is a blueprint check only. Qualified teachers should review item difficulty, ambiguity, and scoring before classroom use; actual learner data are needed to evaluate reliability or outcomes.
