### Licensing boundary

Linking to a publisher's public page is different from obtaining permission to reproduce its audio, video, stills, proprietary transcript or brand in a commercial book. External media remains on its original host. Before commercial use, follow the host's educational-use and copyright terms and obtain any needed permissions for redistributing content. Availability and current playback were not guaranteed from these metadata checks.

### Remaining quality steps

Confirm each question directly against the playable original video, ensure comprehension answer keys and timestamps are accurate, test regional playback accessibility, offer transcripts/captions or a legitimate alternative, and verify the resource index actually opens from Word, PDF and EPUB.
